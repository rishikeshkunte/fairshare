How flatmates split rent and bills without drama

Keep rent, Wi-Fi, groceries, and the maid in one group. Use recurring expenses for the fixed costs and a 1-on-1 ledger for anything that is only between two people.

✈️

Group travel without the spreadsheet

Create one group per trip, keep the local currency, and add expenses as they happen. At the end, simplify debts so everyone makes the fewest possible payments.

🍽️

Restaurant bills: equal, exact, or shares

Equal is fine for a quick lunch. For a big table, use exact amounts or shares so the person who ordered the sharing platter does not subsidise the whole table.

Why avatars make a ledger easier to scan

Names blur together in a long list. A consistent emoji per person makes balances and settlements readable at a glance — and you can change yours any time.
