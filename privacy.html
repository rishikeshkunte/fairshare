Privacy

Your ledger stays yours.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What we store

The app stores your groups, expenses, settlements, contacts, emoji choices, and preferences in your browser’s local storage. If you sign in with Gmail, the same ledger is synced to your Firebase account so your devices can see it.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ad networks.
  • No sale or rental of personal data.
  • No bank credentials or payment card details.

Gmail sign-in

Sign-in is optional. It uses Google OAuth through Firebase Authentication. We use your email and display name to identify you to groups you belong to. We do not read your Gmail messages.

Groups and shared data

When you join a shared group, its members can see the group ledger, the names and emoji avatars shown for members, and your balance in that group. Your other groups are not shared.

Storage and retention

Local data stays on your device until you clear site data. Cloud data stays until you delete it or ask for account deletion. Signing out does not delete your local ledger.

For privacy questions, email fairshare.contact@gmail.com.
