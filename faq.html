FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything about pricing, offline use, sync, import, and privacy.

Is What's My Share? really free?

Yes. It is free while in beta, with no ads and no paywalled core features.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is local-first and installs as a PWA, so you can keep adding expenses without a connection. Changes sync when you are back online.

Does it sync across devices?

Yes. Sign in with Gmail to sync groups, balances, settlements, and emoji choices across devices.

Can I import from Splitwise?

Yes. Import a per-group Splitwise CSV export, including settlements, from the Groups screen.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the app locally first. Sign in only when you want cross-device sync and shared groups.

How do I install it?

Open the app, then use your browser’s install prompt or Share → Add to Home Screen. It behaves like an app and opens offline.

What happens to my data if I sign out?

Your local ledger stays scoped to your account and is not deleted. Cloud data and uploaded receipts are not removed.

Can I change the emoji avatars?

Yes. Tap any person or group emoji to open the picker, choose a new one, or reset it to the default. Choices sync across devices.

Still curious?

Open the app and try a split. No sign-up required.
