Terms

Simple terms for a simple app.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Use of the app

What's My Share? helps you record and split shared expenses. It is not a bank, payment processor, or financial adviser. It does not move money; it records who paid whom.

Your data

You are responsible for the accuracy of the expenses and settlements you enter. You should keep your own records if the data matters to you. You can download your data from the You tab.

Shared groups

When you share a group, other members can see and change its ledger. Only share groups with people you trust.

Availability

The app is provided as-is while in beta. Features may change, and sync depends on your connection and Firebase availability.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use the app for unlawful activity.
  • Do not attempt to access groups or accounts that are not yours.
  • Do not abuse invites, sync, or storage.
