Features

Fast to enter. Easy to trust.

Every screen is built around one question: what's the next thing I need to do? Add the bill, split it your way, and know the next payment.

👥

Groups and 1-on-1 ledgers

Keep trips, the flat, and individual friendships separate. Switch groups in a tap; each ledger keeps its own members and currency.

  • Group picker with emoji chips
  • Friends and contacts hub
  • Separate 1-on-1 balances
🧮

Four ways to split

Equal, exact amounts, percentages, or shares. The breakdown updates as you type, with a live preview of each person's share.

  • Equal split with exclusions
  • Exact paise-level amounts
  • Percentage and ratio splits
🤝

Fewer, clearer payments

Simplify debts to reduce the number of transfers while preserving exact balances. Or turn it off for the original pairwise view.

  • Simplified settlement suggestions
  • Scope by group or 1-on-1
  • Record a payment in the right ledger
🌍

Multi-currency groups

Each group keeps its own currency, so a dollar trip never distorts your rupee flat. Currency is shown on every amount.

🔁

Recurring expenses

Rent, Wi-Fi, and subscriptions can repeat weekly or monthly without re-entering the details.

📥

Splitwise CSV import

Bring a group over with a per-group Splitwise export. The preview detects duplicates and lets you merge or create a copy.

📊

Insights without the noise

A one-line headline shows the total, the top category, and the month-over-month change. Categories, people, and trend views are one tap away.

📲

Installable, offline-first PWA

Add it to your home screen. Keep recording expenses offline; changes sync when you reconnect.

🔐

Privacy by default

Your ledger lives in your browser first. Gmail sign-in is optional and only used to sync groups you belong to.

😎

Emoji avatars

Pick from a large emoji stash for people and groups. Your choices sync across devices.
