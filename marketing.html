FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is What's My Share? really free?

Yes. It is free while in beta, with no ads and no paywalled core features.

Does it sync across devices?

Yes. Sign in with Gmail to sync groups, balances, settlements, and emoji choices across devices.

Can I import from Splitwise?

Yes. Import a per-group Splitwise CSV export, including settlements, from the Groups screen.

Do I need an account?

Yes. What's My Share? currently requires a Gmail account. Sign-in is used to sync your groups across devices and keep your ledger scoped to your account.
